Leader FAQ

The questions you’ll get asked.

Is Resurgens competing with the Buckhead relocation?

No — and this is the most important thing to be able to say clearly. Resurgens is one pillar of the same campaign that includes Buckhead. Buckhead strengthens the center; Resurgens multiplies into the city. They are two parts of one mission, not two campaigns competing for the same dollars or attention. Whenever Resurgens is shown, it appears beside the other pillars — Buckhead, Sandy Springs, Terminus, ministry expansion — precisely so this question answers itself.

Is Resurgens a separate brand or organization?

No. Christ Covenant is the brand; Resurgens is a pillar within it. There is no separate logo suite, domain, social presence, or entity. It’s a restricted fund inside the church, and it lives under the Christ Covenant identity and leadership.

Are Sandy Springs and the other campuses “Resurgens churches”?

No. Every revitalized location is a Christ Covenant campus. Resurgens is the mechanism that recovers the building and plants the campus — it is never the name on the door. Sandy Springs is Christ Covenant Sandy Springs.

How is it governed? Who controls the real estate?

Real-estate and fund decisions are governed by Christ Covenant’s existing bylaws and leadership. No new board, no new nonprofit, no new governance layer. Gifts go into a restricted fund designated for revitalization and are stewarded by the church.

Why revitalize instead of building new?

Cost and speed. A revitalized campus runs about $1.0M; new construction runs roughly $20M — about 20× more. For ~$5.1M, Resurgens recovers five campuses rather than a fraction of one. It also keeps existing buildings in ministry instead of losing them to developers.

What does the money fund, and how much?

The pillar ask is ~$5.1M to recover five campuses at roughly $1.0M each. See The Numbers for the headline proforma; the line-item detail sits in the executive summary and deck.

Is there a public giving page?

Not in Phase 0. There is no separate Resurgens giving portal. Gifts move through Christ Covenant’s existing campaign flow into the restricted fund. Interested partners start a conversation with the generosity team.

Is this site public? Can I share it?

Not yet. This is a gated, noindex Phase 0 site for internal alignment — share the link only with leaders, elders, and key ministry partners. At campaign launch it becomes a subpage of christcovenant.com within the unified campaign frame.
