The story

A building that was almost lost — now full again.

The congregation at Sandy Springs had dwindled to seventeen people. Without intervention, the likeliest outcome was the one playing out across the city: the doors close, the property sells, and a sanctuary becomes something other than a church.

Instead, Christ Covenant stepped in. The same building — revitalized, not rebuilt — became a campus. Within a season, seventeen had become hundreds. Children filled the halls. People were baptized. A neighborhood kept a place that points to God.

The point: this is the entire Resurgens thesis in one campus. Find what’s fading, revitalize it, and watch it multiply — at a fraction of the cost of starting from nothing.
