Resurgens

Strengthen the center. Multiply into the city.

In one sentence: Resurgens is the Christ Covenant pillar that revitalizes dying churches into thriving campuses — about twenty times cheaper than building new.

The problem

Across metro Atlanta, established churches are closing faster than new ones are planted. When a congregation ages out, its building is usually sold — most often to a developer, not another church. The property is lost to ministry permanently, and a neighborhood loses a visible witness on the corner.

The proof — Sandy Springs

Christ Covenant has already run this model once. A fading congregation of 17 became a thriving campus of 720, with 100+ children in ministry and 25 baptisms — in the same building that would otherwise have been sold. This is not a projection; it is a campus you can visit on Sunday.

Fit with the campaign

Resurgens is one pillar of one campaign, shown beside the others — Buckhead, Sandy Springs, Terminus, and ministry expansion. Those pillars strengthen the center; Resurgens multiplies into the city. It is not a competing campaign or a separate brand. Presenting it alongside the other pillars is what dissolves the “two campaigns” confusion.

The model

Find struggling congregations and at-risk buildings → Revitalize them into living Christ Covenant campuses → Multiply the model across the city.

The numbers

$5.1M recovers 5 campuses at roughly $1.0M each — about 20× cheaper than the ~$20M cost of building new. The same dollars reach five locations instead of a fraction of one.

Structure & governance

Resurgens is a restricted fund inside Christ Covenant. Real-estate decisions are governed by the church’s existing bylaws and leadership. No new entity, board, or brand. Every revitalized location is a Christ Covenant campus — never a “Resurgens church.”

The ask

Leaders & influencers: align around the one-sentence story and carry it accurately into every room. Ministry partners: start a conversation with the Christ Covenant generosity team about a gift toward the pillar. Giving runs through the church’s existing campaign flow — there is no separate portal.
