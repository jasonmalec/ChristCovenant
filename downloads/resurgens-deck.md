## Slide 3 — The problem
- Churches are closing faster than they're planted across metro Atlanta.
- Their buildings are usually sold to developers, not other churches.
- Once lost to commercial use, the property almost never returns to ministry.
- A neighborhood loses a visible, local witness on the corner.

---

## Slide 4 — The proof: Sandy Springs
- **17 → 720** weekend attendance, before → after.
- **100+** kids in children's ministry.
- **25** baptisms.
- One building kept in ministry instead of sold.
- *Not a projection — a campus you can visit on Sunday.*

## Slide 6 — The model
**Find → Revitalize → Multiply**
1. **Find** struggling congregations and at-risk buildings before they're lost.
2. **Revitalize** them into living Christ Covenant campuses.
3. **Multiply** the repeatable model across the city.

---

## Slide 7 — The numbers
- **$5.1M** total pillar ask
- **5** campuses recovered
- **~$1.0M** average per campus
- **~20× cheaper** than ~$20M new construction
- For ~$5.1M: 5 campuses vs. less than one built new.

---

## Slide 8 — Structure & governance
- Restricted fund **inside** Christ Covenant.
- Real estate governed by existing **bylaws** and leadership.
- **No new entity, board, or brand.**
- Every campus is a **Christ Covenant** campus — never a "Resurgens church."
