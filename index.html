The problem

Across the city, churches are closing — and the buildings don’t come back.

A congregation ages, attendance thins, and a sanctuary that took generations to build is sold. The most common buyer isn’t another church. It’s a developer. The steeple becomes apartments, a brewery, an office — and a neighborhood quietly loses a place that pointed people to God.

01

Churches are closing faster than they’re planted

Established congregations are aging out, and their properties are going up for sale across metro Atlanta.

02

Buildings are lost to commercial use

When a developer wins, the property almost never returns to ministry. The loss is permanent.

03

Neighborhoods lose a visible witness

A closed church is a gap on the corner — one less place in the community that is noticeably, locally for God.

Multiply into the city

The model

Find. Revitalize. Multiply.

A repeatable playbook we’ve already run once — designed to be run again, campus by campus.

Step 1

Find

Identify struggling congregations and at-risk church buildings before they’re sold to developers and lost to ministry for good.

Step 2

Revitalize

Bring Christ Covenant’s leadership, worship and ministry into the existing building — restoring it as a living, gospel-preaching campus.

Step 3

Multiply

Repeat the model across the city — each revitalized campus strengthening the network and seeding the next.

Structure & governance

Inside the church — not a separate entity.

A restricted fund
Resurgens is a restricted fund inside Christ Covenant. Gifts are designated for revitalization and stewarded by the church — not by an outside organization.
Governed by bylaws
Real-estate decisions are governed by Christ Covenant’s existing bylaws and leadership. There is no new governance layer to build or trust.
No new entity
No new nonprofit, no new board, no new brand. Resurgens lives entirely within the church that already exists, under leadership the congregation already knows.
Christ Covenant campuses
Every revitalized location is a Christ Covenant campus. Sandy Springs and others are not “Resurgens churches” — they are Christ Covenant.
