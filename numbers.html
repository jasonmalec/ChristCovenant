How giving works

One church, one campaign, one giving flow.

There is no separate Resurgens giving portal. Gifts toward this pillar move through Christ Covenant’s existing campaign — into a restricted fund designated for revitalization.

1 · Conversation

Talk with the generosity team

Interested partners start a conversation with Christ Covenant’s generosity team — not a checkout page.

2 · Designation

Give to the restricted fund

Gifts are designated to the Resurgens restricted fund inside the church and governed by existing bylaws.

3 · Stewardship

Deployed campus by campus

Funds are stewarded toward revitalizing campuses as targets are confirmed, with impact reported back.
